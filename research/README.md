# RESEARCH

This directory contains academic research materials, experimental data, and validation studies for the GPU-accelerated optimization project.

## Directory Structure

- **experiments/**: Experimental design and execution by phase
- **datasets/**: Research datasets (TSPLIB, VRPLIB, synthetic)
- **results/**: Experimental results and statistical analysis
- **methodology/**: Research methodology and protocols
- **publications/**: Academic outputs (papers, thesis chapters)
- **TCC_context/**: Original TCC context and requirements

## Purpose

This directory supports academic rigor and reproducible research by providing:

- Structured experimental design and execution
- Standardized datasets for validation and comparison
- Statistical analysis and result visualization
- Academic methodology documentation
- Publication-ready outputs

## Research Standards

All research activities follow:

- Brazilian TCC requirements and standards
- UFSC operational research methodology
- Reproducible research principles
- Statistical validation requirements
- Academic writing and citation standards

The research approach ensures that the implementation meets both educational objectives and academic quality standards for the TCC project.
