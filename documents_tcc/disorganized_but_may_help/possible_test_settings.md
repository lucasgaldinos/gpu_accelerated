# Test settings

## Literature

Voudouris and Tsang used a test setting of:

- **20 problems**
- **48 to 1002 cities**
- **10 runs**

Tüű-Szabó, Földesi and Kóczy used a test setting for VLSI (Very large scale integration) TSP problems of:

- **Intel Pentium Dual CPU T2390 1,86 GHz 2 GB RAM workstation**
- **Instances of up to 20000 nodes**
- **Dataset is available at: <http://www.math.uwaterloo.ca/tsp/vlsi/>**
- **Instances up to 5000 nodes were tested 10 times**
- **Bigger instances were tested 5 times**
