## What

what's the objective of my TCC?

## Why

What's the motivation behind it

## Who

Who will be involved in this project? the orientator, the co-orientator, the company, the client, the people who will be using the project, etc.

## When

When the work will be developed. the cronogram, planning, development proposal.
